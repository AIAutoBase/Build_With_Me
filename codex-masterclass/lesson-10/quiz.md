# Lesson 10: Capstone: build a reviewable workshop launch kit — quiz

## 1. For 36 attended and 48 confirmed, what is attendance rate?

A. 60%
B. 75%
C. 80%
D. 133%

## 2. What do the synthetic attendance results establish?

A. Guaranteed future demand
B. Causal proof of page effectiveness
C. Only the stated properties of the illustrative sample
D. Real Northstar customer performance

## 3. Which brief statement needs official product documentation?

A. We propose a short exercise
B. Our fictional workshop uses three outcomes
C. The synthetic rows total 48 confirmed
D. A claim about current Codex feature availability

## 4. What is an essential handoff distinction?

A. Expected behavior versus actually observed results
B. Long titles versus short titles
C. Blue versus green buttons
D. More files versus fewer files

## 5. A kit scores 9/10 but contains invented test results. Does it pass?

A. Yes, because 9 exceeds 8
B. No, truthful verification is mandatory
C. Yes, if the design looks good
D. Only if the page is published

## Answer key

1. **B — 75%**. Attendance rate uses attended divided by confirmed: 36/48=75%.

2. **C — Only the stated properties of the illustrative sample**. Invented sample data supports arithmetic practice, not real business claims.

3. **D — A claim about current Codex feature availability**. Product availability is a factual claim that needs current support and qualifications.

4. **A — Expected behavior versus actually observed results**. A reviewer must know which checks actually occurred.

5. **B — No, truthful verification is mandatory**. Mandatory conditions cannot be offset by points elsewhere.
