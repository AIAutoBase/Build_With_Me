# Lesson 04 prompt templates

## 10. Bounded research memo

Research a synthetic Northstar pilot using Astra for a workshop plan. Use current official OpenAI sources. Open supporting pages. Separate model guidance, account availability, and tool permissions. Return a short decision memo with claim-level links, access dates, unknowns, and retrieval limits.

## 11. Claim-level source audit

Audit every recommendation against its cited source. Replace unsupported universal claims with the source's actual scope. Label business recommendations as inference. Mark anything not established by the reviewed pages as unknown.

## 12. Research handoff

For this memo, list each material claim, direct official URL, relevant section, access date, and scope. Separate documented facts, inference, and unknowns. Name the next practical check. Do not imply a source was opened unless it was retrieved.
