# Lesson 04: Research with primary sources and evidence checks — quiz

## 1. What should determine the research scope?

A. The longest available article
B. A specific decision and its material claims
C. A fixed number of search results
D. The most popular social post

## 2. A real official link follows an exaggerated claim. Is the claim supported?

A. Always
B. Only if the paragraph is long
C. Only if the linked material supports the exact statement
D. Yes, when Astra wrote it

## 3. Which evidence establishes your own current model access?

A. An unrelated benchmark
B. A general mention of rollout
C. A fictional Northstar scenario
D. Inspection of your available model choices

## 4. A page cannot be retrieved. What is the defensible conclusion?

A. The feature does not exist
B. The page was reviewed successfully
C. Retrieval was incomplete; seek suitable evidence and state the limit
D. The model should invent a likely answer

## 5. How should a business recommendation derived from documentation be labeled?

A. As an interpretation with conditions and supporting evidence
B. As a guaranteed measured result
C. As a quote from the provider
D. As a universal account entitlement

## Answer key

1. **B — A specific decision and its material claims**. A bounded decision tells you which evidence is relevant.

2. **C — Only if the linked material supports the exact statement**. Citation validity depends on the relationship between the source and the claim.

3. **D — Inspection of your available model choices**. General documentation does not establish an individual account's current access.

4. **C — Retrieval was incomplete; seek suitable evidence and state the limit**. An access failure is a retrieval limitation, not evidence of feature absence.

5. **A — As an interpretation with conditions and supporting evidence**. Recommendations involve judgment and should remain distinct from documented facts.
