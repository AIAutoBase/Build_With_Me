# Lesson 06 prompt templates

## 16. Scoped build

Build a local Northstar Workshops page in workshop/. Read guidance and existing files first. Include audience, three outcomes, agenda, and a practice email form. Keep dependencies minimal. Validate inputs and display that nothing was sent or saved. Verify the main button and form. Report files, checks, and limits. Do not publish.

## 17. Visitor journey

Check the local Northstar page: audience clarity, main button, empty and invalid input, learner@example.com, narrow layout, and keyboard navigation. Record expected and observed results. If browser access is unavailable, provide a manual checklist and mark browser behavior unverified.

## 18. Focused correction

The primary button does not reach the form. Inspect its target and the section ID. Make the smallest correction, preserve approved copy and layout, then repeat the check. Report changed files and limitations. Do not publish.
