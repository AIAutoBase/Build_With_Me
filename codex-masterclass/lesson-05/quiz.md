# Lesson 05: CSV analysis and producing useful reports — quiz

## 1. What does one fixture row represent?

A. One unique community member
B. One workshop
C. One registration for one workshop
D. One month of revenue

## 2. What is attendance under the lab definition?

A. 8/12 = 66.67%
B. 8/10 = 80%
C. 10/12 = 83.33%
D. The unweighted average of workshop rates

## 3. How should a blank attendee rating be handled?

A. Convert it to zero
B. Assume it is five
C. Drop the attendee from attendance totals
D. Exclude it from the rating mean and report response coverage

## 4. Which monetary statement does the fixture support?

A. Net ticket collections are $400
B. Profit is $400
C. Profit margin is 80%
D. No refunds occurred

## 5. Why should the report avoid saying Outline Builder caused poor engagement?

A. Its name is too short
B. Astra cannot analyze tables
C. The rows describe outcomes without establishing causes
D. All workshops have identical results

## Answer key

1. **C — One registration for one workshop**. Registration identifiers are unique, but the fixture has no member identifier.

2. **B — 8/10 = 80%**. The denominator is confirmed registrations, excluding two cancellations.

3. **D — Exclude it from the rating mean and report response coverage**. Missing feedback is unknown, while attendance remains observed.

4. **A — Net ticket collections are $400**. $480 initially collected minus $80 refunded equals $400; expenses are absent.

5. **C — The rows describe outcomes without establishing causes**. Small descriptive groups and missing causal information do not support that conclusion.
