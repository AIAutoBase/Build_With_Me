# Lesson 02: Briefing Astra: goals, context, constraints, planning, and steering — quiz

## 1. Which goal is easiest to review?

A. Help our community grow
B. Make something impressive
C. Draft a 30-minute beginner session plan with facilitator notes
D. Improve everything about the launch

## 2. Which context most directly changes the teaching approach?

A. Learners have never taught a workshop
B. The business owner likes long documents
C. The request was typed on Tuesday
D. The folder name is short

## 3. Which done-when criterion is observable?

A. It feels premium
B. Everyone will love it
C. It guarantees participation
D. The agenda totals 30 minutes and includes a worked example

## 4. When is planning especially useful?

A. Only after publication
B. When wrong assumptions would create substantial rework
C. Whenever you want the longest answer
D. Only for programmers

## 5. What should happen when requested activities exceed the fixed session length?

A. Hide the total
B. Delete the time constraint silently
C. Surface the conflict and choose a tradeoff
D. Claim both requirements passed

## Answer key

1. **C — Draft a 30-minute beginner session plan with facilitator notes**. A named deliverable, audience, and format create an inspectable assignment.

2. **A — Learners have never taught a workshop**. Learner experience affects language, pacing, examples, and practice.

3. **D — The agenda totals 30 minutes and includes a worked example**. Timing and the presence of a relevant worked example can be checked.

4. **B — When wrong assumptions would create substantial rework**. Planning exposes important choices before the agent expands the deliverable.

5. **C — Surface the conflict and choose a tradeoff**. Contradictory requirements need an explicit decision and consistent revision.
