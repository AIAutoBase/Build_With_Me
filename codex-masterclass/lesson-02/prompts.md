# Lesson 02 prompt templates

## 4. Four-part session brief

Goal: draft a 30-minute Northstar workshop plan. Context: synthetic beginner creators; output is one promise and three-part outline. Constraints: plain English, no income claims, no publishing. Done when: timings total 30; facilitator notes, worked example, exercise, and assumptions included.

## 5. Plan before drafting

Before drafting, propose the agenda with durations, assumptions, and the two decisions most likely to affect learner success. Check the total. Keep the plan brief and identify any question that must be answered before writing.

## 6. Steer a focused revision

Revise the plan for learners unfamiliar with a workshop promise. Add a plain-English definition and before-and-after example. Keep the session at 30 minutes and preserve the drafting exercise. Report timing changes and rerun the acceptance checks.
