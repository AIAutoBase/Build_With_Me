# Capstone review rubric

Submit a Northstar Workshops launch kit or a comparable small project. Include the final files and enough evidence for another learner to check your reasoning.

## Submission checklist

- A brief defining the audience, deliverables, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- A local landing page with truthful practice behavior.
- A short analysis of the synthetic workshop CSV, including exclusions and denominators.
- A research brief with claims linked to sources and unresolved questions identified.
- A handoff describing actual checks, observed outcomes, and limitations.

## Suggested scoring

These are course-designed assessment criteria, not an external certification standard.

| Criterion | Full-credit evidence | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Scope control | Clear deliverables, inputs, boundaries, and completion checks | 2 |
| Page behavior | Page meets the brief and relevant boundaries have observed test results | 2 |
| Analysis correctness | Validated rows, correct arithmetic, and explicit denominators | 2 |
| Source support | Traceable claims, synthetic data identified, unknowns preserved | 2 |
| Handoff reproducibility | Files are organized, reproduction instructions work, limitations are explicit | 2 |

Completion threshold: 8/10, with no unresolved material factual error or misleading claim that a practice action sent data or created a real reservation. Synthetic data must be labeled, arithmetic must match the supplied rows, and checks must describe actual observations. Do not publish or send messages without authorization.

For each criterion, award 2 when the requirement is met with inspectable evidence, 1 when present but incomplete or weakly verified, and 0 when missing or materially incorrect. Explain deductions with a specific example.

## Peer review questions

1. Can I identify which facts came from the brief?
2. Can I reproduce the important calculation or test?
3. Does the final behavior match what the page or report claims?
4. Are external actions and remaining unknowns clearly described?
5. What single improvement would make this easier to trust or maintain?

Revise the submission after feedback and preserve the checks for the revised version.
