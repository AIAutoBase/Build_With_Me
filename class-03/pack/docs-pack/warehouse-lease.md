# Commercial Lease Agreement — Unit 12B, Keystone Industrial Park

**Landlord:** Keystone Property Group
**Tenant:** Ridgeline Contracting LLC
**Premises:** Unit 12B, approximately 4,200 square feet

## Term and rent

Initial term is **36 months**, commencing 1 June 2026 and ending 31 May 2029.

Base rent is **$3,850 per month**, due on the first of each month. Rent increases
by **3% annually** on each anniversary of the commencement date. A late fee of
$150 applies to rent received after the fifth of the month.

Security deposit is $7,700, equal to two months of base rent.

## Renewal and termination

Tenant may renew for one additional 36-month term by giving written notice
**not less than 120 days** before expiration. Early termination requires 90 days
written notice and forfeits the entire security deposit.

## Use and maintenance

Premises may be used for contracting operations, equipment storage and light
fabrication. Tenant maintains the interior and all HVAC serving the unit.
Landlord maintains the roof, exterior walls and parking area.

Tenant may not sublet without written landlord consent.
