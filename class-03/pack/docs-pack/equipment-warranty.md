# Limited Warranty — Vulcan VX-900 Commercial Dishwasher

**Model:** VX-900 · **Serial:** VX9-2026-88213
**Purchased:** 14 April 2026 from Restaurant Supply Depot

## What is covered

Vulcan warrants this unit against defects in materials and workmanship for
**three years** from the date of purchase on all parts, and **five years** on the
stainless steel wash tank and door assembly.

Labor is covered for the **first twelve months only**. After twelve months the
owner is responsible for service call and labor charges even where the failed
part itself is still under warranty.

## What is not covered

Damage from water hardness above 10 grains per gallon is not covered. Vulcan
requires a water softener where hardness exceeds that level; failure to install
one voids the wash tank coverage.

Consumable items - door gaskets, wash arm bearings, detergent lines - are
excluded. Damage from improper voltage or from installation by a non-certified
technician is excluded.

## Service

Authorized service only. Call 1-888-555-0142 with the serial number. Unauthorized
repair voids the remaining warranty.
