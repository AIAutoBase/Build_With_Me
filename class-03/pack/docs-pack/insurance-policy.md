# Commercial General Liability Policy — Northgate Mutual

**Policy number:** CGL-4471-B
**Insured:** Ridgeline Contracting LLC
**Period:** 1 March 2026 to 1 March 2027

## Coverage limits

General aggregate limit is $2,000,000. Each-occurrence limit is $1,000,000.
Products and completed operations aggregate is $1,000,000.
Damage to premises rented to you is capped at $100,000 per occurrence.
Medical expense limit is $10,000 per person.

## Deductible

The property damage deductible is **$2,500 per occurrence**. Bodily injury claims
carry no deductible. The deductible applies before any payment is issued and is
the responsibility of the insured.

## Exclusions

This policy does not cover work performed at heights above 30 feet without a
filed supplemental endorsement. Damage arising from faulty workmanship on the
insured's own completed work is excluded. Pollution liability is excluded.

## Claims

Report claims within 30 days of discovery by calling 1-800-555-0177 or through
the broker of record. Late notice may result in denial of the claim.
