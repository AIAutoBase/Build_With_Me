Build a one-page website for blackcatmom.com in a new folder named blackcatmom-site/ inside the current directory.

GOAL
A fast, elegant, accessible one-page site about cats, with the emphasis on BLACK cats. It has a hero video, black cat fun facts, three Amazon affiliate shop sections, a follow-us section, a community signup placeholder and a footer. This is a STAGING build. Do not deploy anything. Do not run git commands.

STACK RULES
- Plain HTML, CSS and a tiny vanilla JS file only if needed. No framework. No build step. No npm. No CDN scripts.
- Files: blackcatmom-site/index.html, blackcatmom-site/style.css, blackcatmom-site/script.js (keep under ~60 lines), blackcatmom-site/assets/ for media.
- Mobile-first CSS. Use CSS custom properties for colors. System font stack, or one Google Font loaded with display=swap (optional, max one family).
- The page must open correctly by double-clicking index.html (relative paths only).

FILES THE PAGE REFERENCES (they may not exist yet - they are made separately)
- assets/hero.mp4 and assets/hero-poster.jpg (hero video and its still frame)
- assets/img/facts.jpg, assets/img/toys.jpg, assets/img/food.jpg, assets/img/health.jpg, assets/img/community.jpg
- assets/og-image.jpg (1200x630, for social previews)
- assets/favicon.svg (YOU create this: a simple, clean black cat silhouette with two gold eyes, SVG, readable at 16px)
Placeholders: create assets/ and assets/img/ folders. Do NOT create fake binary files. Instead, style every image container with a background color and gradient in the night palette so the layout still looks finished when an image file is missing. Give every <img> width and height attributes and loading="lazy" (except the hero poster). Keep the hero readable with no video and no poster: dark gradient background behind the headline.

VISUAL DIRECTION
- Elegant, warm, night palette. Not spooky, not cartoon Halloween.
- Colors: deep charcoal background (around #141317), soft off-black panels (around #1e1c22), warm cream text (around #f3ede2), gold accent like a cat's eyes (around #d9a441), muted amber for hover. Check every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text). Adjust shades if they fail.
- Generous spacing, rounded corners (8-12px), subtle shadows, one small inline SVG cat icon used as the logo mark and as section dividers. No emoji anywhere on the page.
- Gold focus rings that are clearly visible.

SECTIONS (in this order, each a <section> with an id and an <h2>)

1. Header: SVG cat logo mark + "Black Cat Mom" wordmark. Small nav with anchor links: Facts, Shop, Follow, Community. On mobile the nav can wrap or scroll horizontally; no hamburger JS needed.

2. Hero (id="top")
- Full-width background video: <video autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="metadata" poster="assets/hero-poster.jpg"> with <source src="assets/hero.mp4" type="video/mp4">. The video is decorative: aria-hidden="true", no controls attribute.
- Dark overlay gradient so the headline always passes contrast.
- Headline: "For the love of black cats"
- Subline: "Fun facts, good toys, and care tips for the house panther in your life."
- CTA button: "Shop cat favorites" linking to #shop. Second, quieter link: "Read the fun facts" to #facts.
- A visible pause/play button for the video (WCAG 2.2.2: moving content longer than 5 seconds needs a pause control). Label it with aria-label and aria-pressed. This is the only reason script.js exists.
- prefers-reduced-motion: reduce -> in script.js, pause the video and do not autoplay; in CSS, hide the video and show the poster image as the background. Also turn off any CSS transitions/animations under reduced motion.

3. Black cat fun facts (id="facts")
- Intro line: "A few true things about black cats, plus some famous folklore. Folklore is labeled."
- Render each fact as a card. Folklore cards get a small visible tag that says "Folklore". Use these facts word for word (light edits for flow only, do not add new facts, do not add numbers):
  a) The Bombay breed is always black. Breeders developed it in the United States in the 1950s.
  b) Black is not a breed. Many different cat breeds can have a solid black coat, and so can mixed-breed cats.
  c) A black cat's color comes from a dark pigment called eumelanin.
  d) Strong sun can fade a black coat to a rusty or brownish shade.
  e) In bright light, some black cats show faint "ghost" tabby stripes under the black.
  f) Many black cats have gold, copper or green eyes. Against black fur, they really stand out.
  g) Black Cat Appreciation Day is celebrated in the United States on August 17.
  h) National Black Cat Day is held in the UK on October 27. The charity Cats Protection started it.
  i) [Folklore] In the UK and Ireland, a black cat crossing your path is often said to bring good luck.
  j) [Folklore] In Japan, black cats are traditionally seen as a sign of good fortune.
  k) [Folklore] Sailors once kept black cats on ships for luck, and they were good at catching mice on board.
  l) [Folklore] Old European superstitions linked black cats with witches. That is part of why they became a Halloween symbol. Many shelters now run black cat adoption events to show how sweet they are.
- No medical claims anywhere in this section.
- Optional decorative image: assets/img/facts.jpg with alt="A black cat with gold eyes resting on a windowsill at dusk".

ACCESSIBILITY CHECKLIST (build it in, then verify)
- One <h1> (the hero headline), then <h2> per section, <h3> per shop category.
- Skip link "Skip to content" as the first focusable element.
- Landmarks: header, nav, main, footer.
- All meaningful images have the alt text given above; decorative SVGs have aria-hidden="true".
- Visible focus styles on every link, button and input.
- Links that open a new tab: add visually hidden text " (opens in a new tab)".
- Touch targets at least 44x44px.
- prefers-reduced-motion respected as described in the hero.
